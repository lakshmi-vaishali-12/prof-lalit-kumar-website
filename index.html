01 / ABOUT
A CAREER IN ENGINEERING

Knowledge built
over decades.

Prof. Lalit Kumar is an engineering academic and microwave electronics specialist whose career spans research, technology development, institutional leadership and teaching.

He is associated with the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus. His professional work has focused on microwave vacuum electronic devices, vacuum microelectronics and computational electromagnetics.
