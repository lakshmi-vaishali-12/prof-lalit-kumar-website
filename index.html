01 / ABOUT

Engineering ideas.
Real-world impact.

Dr. Lalit Kumar is an academic and microwave electronics specialist with a career spanning scientific research, technology development, institutional leadership and teaching.

He is currently Professor of Practice in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus. His professional interests centre on microwave vacuum electronic devices, vacuum microelectronics and computational electromagnetics.

Over several decades, his work has covered the theory, simulation and engineering development of microwave tubes and high-power microwave systems, alongside national and international collaborative projects.

01 / DEVICES↗

Microwave Vacuum Electronic Devices

Design, analysis and development of coaxial magnetrons, helix and coupled-cavity TWTs, multi-beam klystrons and gyrotrons.
