01 — About

A life in
engineering.

Professor Lalit Kumar is an engineering academic whose professional work is associated with microwave electronics, vacuum electronic devices and electromagnetic engineering.

He is affiliated with the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus. This profile presents an overview of his academic interests and professional work.
